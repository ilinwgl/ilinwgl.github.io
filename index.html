    Guanlin Wang

    Computer Vision · AI Engineering · Software Development

    Welcome to my personal technical website.

    I'm a Software Engineer with 5+ years of professional experience in Computer Vision and AI application development. Throughout my career, I have designed and delivered production-ready solutions across a wide range of computer vision domains, including OCR, Object Detection, Feature Matching, Visual Localization, 3D Geometry Analysis, and Industrial QR & Barcode Recognition.

    My interests extend beyond individual AI models to the design of complete AI systems. I focus on building production-ready solutions that integrate model development, deployment, scalable backend services, and intelligent agent frameworks. In recent years, I have been actively exploring Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, and modern software architecture to develop modular, extensible, and maintainable AI platforms.

    This website serves as my long-term technical knowledge base, where I document my learning, engineering practices, project experience, and technical experiments. My goal is not only to record what I build, but also to understand the underlying principles, summarize practical solutions, and share reusable knowledge through well-structured documentation.

    I believe that great software is built through continuous learning, thoughtful design, and persistent iteration. Every note and project on this site reflects a step in my journey as an engineer.

    An AI Agent platform designed for Computer Vision applications.

    CVHub integrates Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and a microservice-based architecture to build intelligent, extensible, and production-ready AI systems.

    Core components include:

    • AI Agent
    • LLM Integration
    • RAG System
    • Workflow Planning
    • Microservice Architecture
    • Tool & Skill Management
    • Computer Vision Services
    • REST-API & gRPC Communication

    Engineering Principles

    The projects and notes on this website generally follow several principles:

    • Keep modules independent and responsibilities clear.
    • Prefer explicit and readable workflows.
    • Separate model inference from business logic.
    • Design reusable pipelines instead of isolated scripts.
    • Use stable interfaces between services.
    • Document important technical decisions and trade-offs.
    • Treat deployment and maintenance as part of the system design.
